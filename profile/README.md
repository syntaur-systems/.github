  ## Personal AI platform — one home for the services people already use.

  Smart home, finances, scheduling, voice, communications — most people stitch these together across a dozen apps and accounts. Syntaur consolidates them under one consistent interface, with a persistent memory layer so the AI actually remembers you.

  ---

  ### What we build

  **Pure-Rust gateway** for the household&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;multi-protocol (Matter, Thread, BLE, MQTT, vendor APIs), no vendor cloud dependencies

  **Tax & finance**&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;year-round picture with pass-through partner filing

  **Scheduler**&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;calendar, todos, habits in one coherent view

  **Voice**&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;local STT and AI assistance via Bluetooth pendant or phone

  **Privacy**&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;per-device policy, DNS sinkhole, network segmentation

  **Engine**&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;the pure-Rust runtime that everything else builds on

  ---

  ### Operating principles

  **Local-first.**&nbsp;&nbsp;Your home runs on your gateway, not our cloud.

  **Pure Rust.**&nbsp;&nbsp;Every service we own is Rust.

  **Pass-through partnerships.**&nbsp;&nbsp;Third-party services routed at cost, no markup.

  **Open by design.**&nbsp;&nbsp;Standards (Matter, MQTT, IMAP) over proprietary lock-in.

  **One identity.**&nbsp;&nbsp;Sign in once, everywhere.
